Hunting Dog GPS Tracker for the UK — No Subscription, No SIM

Track your gundogs, lurchers, and working dogs across British moorland, woodland, and shooting estates without a SIM card or monthly fee.

Most hunting dog trackers are cellular devices. They work well near villages, but during a day's shoot across the Scottish Highlands, Pennines, Welsh valleys, or North York Moors, the signal drops and so does your dog's location.

Loko is different. It uses LoRa radio on the 868 MHz band — the same licence-free frequency used across the UK — to transmit GPS positions directly to your handheld receiver. No SIM card, no monthly contract, no mobile coverage needed.

UK Regulations & Compatibility

Loko operates on the 868 MHz ISM band, which is licence-free in the UK under Ofcom regulations. No radio licence is required to own or operate the device.

The device complies with UKCA and CE marking requirements. It ships to the UK with no import restrictions.

Unlike cellular trackers, Loko has no roaming charges, no UK carrier dependency, and works identically whether you are in the Scottish Highlands, New Forest, Norfolk Broads, or Yorkshire Dales.
