Loko GPS Tracker — Setup Guide

Get your first position on the map: prepare Loko Ground, charge both units, connect your phone and turn on Loko Air. It takes about 15 minutes.

Overview

Loko is an offline GPS tracker. Loko Air finds its position and sends it over LoRa radio. Loko Ground receives the position and forwards it to your phone over Bluetooth, and the Loko app shows it on the map. No SIM card, account or subscription is needed.

Note: Loko works with its default settings. To change timing, GNSS mode, radio settings or tracker IDs, or to export tracks, see the full user manual (PDF).

Before you start

  • The Loko app on your phone: iPhone with iOS 15 or later, or Android 11 or later. Download it from the software page.
  • A Li-ion 18650 button-top battery for Loko Ground. Any capacity works; a higher capacity runs longer on one charge.
  • A + (cross-tip) screwdriver for the 4 screws on the back of Loko Ground.
  • A 5 V USB charger (any phone charger) and a USB-C cable for charging.
  • For the firmware update: a computer with Chrome or Edge and a USB-C data cable. A charge-only cable cannot transfer data.

Step 1 — Prepare Loko Ground

  1. Remove the 4 screws on the back of Loko Ground with the + (cross-tip) screwdriver, then lift off the back cover.
Warning: Check the battery orientation before you insert it. Reversed polarity can damage the protection chip on the circuit board.
  1. Insert the Li-ion 18650 button-top battery. The negative (−) end goes toward the antenna, and the positive (+) end goes at the opposite end.
  1. Update the Loko Ground firmware while the cover is off. Follow Update Loko Ground firmware, then come back to step 4.
  1. Put the back cover back on and tighten the 4 screws.

Step 2 — Charge both units

  1. Connect Loko Air to a 5 V USB charger with a USB-C cable. Charging starts automatically. When the battery is full, the red LED lights solid.
  2. Charge Loko Ground the same way. Loko Ground has no charging indicator.
Note: If Loko Air is switched on while it charges, the LED gives a short blink every 5 seconds until the battery is full. This is normal.

Step 3 — Connect Loko Ground to your phone

  1. Turn on Loko Ground: press and hold Power. The LED lights green, then red, then blue, and then goes out. Release the button when the LED goes out. The LED then starts blinking blue: Loko Ground is ready to connect.
  2. Open the Loko app and allow the permissions it asks for. Location shows your own position. Bluetooth (called Nearby devices on Android) lets the app find Loko Ground. Allow Notifications if you want geofence alerts and chimes while the app is closed.
  3. Tap the menu icon (☰) in the top-left corner. The menu opens.
  4. Tap Bluetooth, then tap Scan.
  5. Tap Connect next to LOKO. The Loko Ground LED turns solid blue when it connects to your phone.
  6. Tap the map to close the menu.
Check: Solid blue means Loko Ground is connected to your phone. It does not mean a position from Loko Air has arrived yet.

Step 4 — Turn on Loko Air outdoors

  1. Take Loko Air outside, to a place with a clear view of the sky.
  2. Press and hold the Loko Air button for about 4 seconds, until the solid red LED goes out. Release the button. Loko Air is now on and looks for its first position, which can take longer the first time.
  • Turn off Loko Air: press and hold the button for about 4 seconds, until the blinking red LED goes out.
  • Check whether it is on: press the button briefly, for 1–2 seconds. If the LED blinks, Loko Air is on. If it lights solid, Loko Air is off. A press shorter than 3 seconds does not change the state.
  • Turn off Loko Ground: press and hold Power until the LED turns red, then release it.

Step 5 — Find your tracker on the map

  1. Wait for a round marker with the tracker's ID1 on it. It is red by default, and it appears when the app receives a position from Loko Air.
  2. Tap the marker to see the tracker's details: position, battery, time of the last position and distance from you.
Check: The ID1 marker confirms that a position travelled from Loko Air through Loko Ground to the app.
Tip: Before you go out of internet coverage, download maps of your area: menu → Offline Maps → + Download New Zone. Tracking works without internet, but the map only shows areas you have downloaded. Offline, select the Map Style that matches the map source you downloaded.

Troubleshooting

Start with the first check in each row; the later checks take longer.

What you seeWhat to check
Loko Ground does not appear in the Bluetooth scan1. Loko Ground is on: its LED blinks blue.
2. Bluetooth is on in your phone.
3. The Loko app is allowed to use Bluetooth (Nearby devices on Android).
4. Loko Ground is charged. It has no charging indicator, so charge it if unsure.
Loko Ground is connected (solid blue) but Loko Air is not on the map1. Loko Air is on: a short press makes its LED blink.
2. Loko Air is outdoors with a clear view of the sky; the first position can take longer.
3. Enough time has passed for a send: Loko Air sends on every N-th wake-up (Send Mult), and Send Mult is not 0.
4. The Daily Sleep Window is not active right now (times are UTC).
5. Transmit Mode is P2P.
6. Frequency, ID2 and the encryption key match on both units.
The map is blank when you are offline1. You downloaded this area before going offline.
2. Map Style matches the map source you downloaded.
3. If the app reported missing tiles after the download, download the same area again.
Geofence alerts do not arrive while the app is closedNotifications are allowed for the Loko app.
Loko Air or Loko Ground is missing from the USB port list1. The cable is a USB-C data cable, not a charge-only cable.
2. You are using Chrome or Edge.
3. Loko Ground is switched on before you connect it.

Update firmware

Keep Loko Air and Loko Ground on the latest firmware. You need a computer with Chrome or Edge, a USB-C data cable and the Loko Configuration Tool at nolilab.com/loko-config-tool/. Nothing to install.

Update Loko Air firmware

  1. Open the Loko Configuration Tool: nolilab.com/loko-config-tool/
  2. Connect Loko Air to the computer with the USB-C data cable.
  3. Click Firmware Update, then Loko Air.
  4. Click Connect, then select the Loko Air port in the window that opens.
  5. Under Flash, select Latest, then click Flash Firmware.
  6. Wait until the progress bar reaches 100% and the tool shows Flash complete. Do not unplug Loko Air before that.
  7. Unplug Loko Air and plug it back in to restart it.

Update Loko Ground firmware

Before you start, take off the back cover (4 screws) and install the Li-ion 18650 button-top battery. You will use three buttons on the board: Power (the big button), IO and EN (RESET).

  1. Connect Loko Ground to the computer with the USB-C data cable.
  2. Open the Loko Configuration Tool: nolilab.com/loko-config-tool/
  3. Click Firmware Update, then Loko Ground.
  4. Press and hold Power (the big button). Keep holding it until step 9.
  5. While you hold Power, press and hold IO.
  6. While you hold both IO and Power, press EN (RESET) once.
  7. Release IO. Keep holding Power.
  8. Click Flash Firmware, then select the serial port in the window that opens.
  9. When the progress bar reaches 100% and shows Done, release Power.
  10. Press EN (RESET) once.
  11. Put the back cover back on and tighten the 4 screws.
Check: The update is complete when the progress bar showed Done at 100% and you pressed EN (RESET).

Warnings

BATTERY

Check the 18650 polarity before you insert it: negative (−) toward the antenna. Reversed polarity can damage the circuit board.

WATERPROOF

Not waterproof, but withstands light rain. Do not submerge it. Always cap the USB port on the Air unit.

TEMPERATURE

Operate within −10 °C to +60 °C.

VOLTAGE

Charge at 5 V only. Higher voltages will destroy the device.

FIRE

Do not expose to open flame or extreme heat.

RF

Do not enclose in metal. Keep away from strong EM sources. Make sure your frequency and TX power are allowed where you use Loko.

ANTENNA

Mount the antenna pointing upward, as high from the ground as possible.

EYE SAFETY

Keep the antenna tip away from children's and animals' eyes.
