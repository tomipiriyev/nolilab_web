Built for where cell towers end

Loko is the world's smallest LoRa GPS tracker. At just 22 × 22 × 15 mm and 14 grams, it attaches to anything — a dog collar, a drone frame, farm machinery, a backpack strap — and keeps transmitting your exact location for up to 12 months on a single charge.

Unlike every cellular GPS tracker on the market, Loko uses LoRa P2P radio to communicate directly with your Ground Station over 20 km of open terrain. No SIM card, no data plan, no monthly bill. Ever.

The companion app for iOS and Android displays real-time position, tracking history, geofence alerts, and manages up to 30 simultaneous devices. Your data stays on your device — no cloud required.

All firmware is open source. Advanced users can extend, modify, and reflash over USB-C.
