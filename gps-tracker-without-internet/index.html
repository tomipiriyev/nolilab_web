GPS Tracker Without Internet

Track location where phones have no data signal and cloud trackers stop updating.

Internet access is not required for GPS location. GPS satellites broadcast positioning signals globally. The internet is only used by many trackers to relay the location back to an app.

Loko is built for offline tracking. The tracker receives satellite position and sends it over LoRa radio to your handheld receiver, so location updates can continue in places with no Wi-Fi, no mobile data, and no cloud connection.

What Offline GPS Tracking Means

Offline GPS tracking means the device can calculate and share position without relying on the internet. GPS provides coordinates. A local radio link carries those coordinates to the person doing the tracking.

This is different from offline maps. Offline maps help your phone display terrain without mobile data. Loko adds the missing piece: a radio link from the tracked object back to you.

Best Places to Use a GPS Tracker Without Internet

Offline tracking fits forests, farms, mountain trails, drone flight fields, rural properties, construction sites, search areas, and any location where cellular service is weak.

The practical range depends on terrain, antenna placement, and line of sight. Open terrain performs best. Hills, buildings, dense vegetation, and low antenna placement reduce range.

Limitations to Understand

Loko is not a satellite messenger and does not send your location to someone across the world by itself. It is a local long-range radio tracking system.

For emergency communication far beyond local radio range, a satellite communicator is the right category. For local recovery and asset tracking without internet, Loko is the better fit.

FAQ

Can GPS work without internet?

Yes. GPS satellites work without internet. Internet is only needed by apps or trackers that depend on cloud data transfer.

Can Loko show location if my phone has no signal?

Yes. Loko Ground receives the tracker over LoRa and connects to your phone by Bluetooth.

Does Loko need Wi-Fi?

No. Loko P2P tracking does not require Wi-Fi.

Is this the same as a satellite tracker?

No. Loko is a local LoRa radio GPS tracker, not a satellite messaging device.

Loko GPS Tracker uses GPS plus LoRa radio for local tracking without SIM cards, internet, or monthly subscriptions.
