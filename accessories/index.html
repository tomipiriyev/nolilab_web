3D-Printed Accessories

Mounts, clips and stands for the Loko GPS tracker — free CAD files you can print at home. Print them as-is, or open the source model and adapt it to your collar, harness or airframe.

Collar Mount

Straps the Loko Air to a dog collar up to 40 mm wide. The tracker slides in and is fixed with 4 screws.

Loko Air · ~9 g · 2 h print Download CAD files

Drone Mount

Low-profile cradle that zip-ties to a drone frame or landing gear, keeping the antenna clear of carbon fibre so the tracker still reports after a hard landing.

Loko Air · ~7 g · 1.5 h print File coming soon

Belt / Backpack Clip

Spring clip for a belt, backpack strap or pack lid. Useful when you're carrying the tracker yourself on a hike rather than mounting it on an animal.

Loko Air · ~11 g · 2 h print File coming soon

Ground Unit Enclosure

A rugged printed housing for the Loko Ground unit. Print it if you want a tougher shell around your base station out in the field.

Loko Ground · rugged enclosure File coming soon

Suggested print settings

  • MaterialPETG or ASA for outdoor use — PLA warps in a car or in direct summer sun.
  • Layer height0.2 mm is plenty; 0.15 mm if you want cleaner clip surfaces.
  • Infill30–40 % — these parts are small and take real load.
  • Walls3 perimeters minimum, so clips flex without cracking.
