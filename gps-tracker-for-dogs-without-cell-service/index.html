GPS Tracker for Dogs Without Cell Service

A local dog tracking option for farms, trails, hunting areas, campsites, and rural land where cellular pet trackers stop updating.

Most dog GPS trackers are cellular devices. They can work well in town, but they need a mobile network to send location back to the app. When your dog runs into forest, farmland, hills, or a no-signal valley, the tracker may only show an old location.

Loko uses a different model: GPS for position and LoRa radio for local communication. The tracker sends location directly to a nearby receiver, so you can track locally without cell service, SIM cards, or per-dog subscriptions.

Why Cell Service Matters for Dog Trackers

The GPS chip in a dog tracker can calculate location without cellular coverage. The weak point is the return path. If the tracker sends that location through LTE or GSM, the app stops receiving live updates when the device loses towers.

This is exactly the problem people run into with rural dogs, working dogs, hunting dogs, and dogs that roam across large properties. The dog may still be nearby, but the cellular tracker cannot report its current position.

Where Loko Fits

Loko is best when the handler is in the same broad area as the dog and needs local recovery. Examples include farms, cabins, training fields, hiking routes, campsites, search areas, and rural homes with poor carrier coverage.

It is not a cloud tracker for checking a dog from another city. If nobody nearby has the receiver, a cellular tracker or satellite product is the right category. Loko is built for local off-grid tracking.

FAQ

Can a dog GPS tracker work without cell service?

Yes, if it uses a non-cellular link such as LoRa radio to send GPS coordinates to a local receiver.

Does Loko charge per dog?

No. Loko does not use cellular data plans, so there is no per-dog monthly tracking subscription.

Is Loko a dog collar?

Loko is a compact GPS tracker. It should be attached with a secure mount appropriate for the dog's size, activity, and environment.

Is AirTag enough for dogs?

AirTag can help near people and Apple devices, but it is not a GPS tracker and is unreliable in remote areas.
