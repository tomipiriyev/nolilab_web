GPS Tracker for Dogs Without Cell Service

Five ways to keep track of a dog where phones show no signal, and how to choose between them.

Most dog GPS trackers send the dog's position over the mobile network. When your dog runs into forest, farmland or a valley with no signal, the app stops updating and shows an old position.

There are five ways to keep tracking without cell service. This page compares them and says plainly where Loko is the wrong choice.

How to Choose

  1. Will you be in the same area as your dog? If not, only a cellular tracker can reach you, and only where there is coverage.
  2. Does your dog swim? Choose a collar with a published water rating. Loko is not waterproof.
  3. Do you need training features? Dogtra and Garmin offer them. Loko only tracks.
  4. Handheld or phone? A handheld keeps working when your phone battery is flat.
  5. How many dogs, and what budget? See the cost example below.

Where Loko Is the Wrong Choice

  • Dogs that swim or retrieve from water.
  • Dogs that might travel beyond radio range of your receiver.
  • Owners who want to check on the dog from another town.
  • Training with stimulation, tone or vibration.

Know Loko's Limits

  • You must be within radio range of your Loko Ground: 20+ km in open areas with line of sight, much less among trees, hills and buildings.
  • If the tracker cannot get a satellite fix within 60 seconds, it sends its last known position. The app shows when each position arrived but no warning that it is old, so check the time.
  • Out of range, Loko Air keeps logging up to 3,100 positions on the device (oldest overwritten first). The Ground stores positions while your phone is disconnected, but the app does not pull them in when you reconnect.
  • Loko is not waterproof; it withstands light rain only. The Ground runs on an 18650 battery, which is not included.
  • Battery life depends on the update interval: up to 1 year at long intervals, much less at short ones.

FAQ

Can a dog GPS tracker work without cell service?

Yes, if it sends the position by radio to a receiver you carry, as hunting collars and Loko do. Cellular trackers stop updating without coverage.

Does Loko charge per dog?

No. Each extra Loko Air tracker is a one-time $59, and one Loko Ground follows up to 30 trackers.

Is Loko waterproof?

No. It withstands light rain only, so it is not suitable for dogs that swim.

Is AirTag enough for dogs?

Near people and Apple devices it can help, but it has no GPS and is unreliable in remote areas.
