Livestock GPS Tracker With No Cell Service

Four ways to track animals where there is no mobile signal, and where Loko fits.

Tracking livestock gets expensive when every animal needs a mobile plan, and it fails where there is no coverage. This page compares the four main approaches and says plainly where Loko is the wrong choice.

Four Approaches Compared

ApproachNo cell service?Running costMain limit
Cellular ear tags or collarsNoPer-animal planNeeds coverage on the land
Satellite tagsYesUsually a per-device planCost per animal
LoRaWAN gateway tagsYesGateway and network setupYou must install and maintain gateways
Carry-your-own-receiver LoRa (Loko)YesNoneYou see animals only when within range; not waterproof

Where Loko Fits

Loko suits finding animals or equipment on your own land when you go out with the receiver. One Loko Ground follows up to 30 trackers. A bundle is $119 and each extra tracker $59, so ten animals cost $650 in hardware with no running fee (an illustration, not a quote).

Where Loko Is the Wrong Choice

  • Animals outdoors in all weather. Loko is not waterproof; it withstands light rain only.
  • Constant monitoring from the house. Loko reports to the receiver you carry, not to a server.
  • Large herds. One receiver follows up to 30 trackers.
  • Ear tags. Loko has no ear-tag mount.

Know Loko's Limits

  • You must be within radio range of your Loko Ground: 20+ km in open areas with line of sight, much less among trees, hills and buildings.
  • If the tracker cannot get a satellite fix within 60 seconds, it sends its last known position. The app shows when each position arrived but no warning that it is old, so check the time.
  • Out of range, Loko Air keeps logging up to 3,100 positions on the device (oldest overwritten first). The Ground stores positions while your phone is disconnected, but the app does not pull them in when you reconnect.
  • Loko is not waterproof; it withstands light rain only. The Ground runs on an 18650 battery, which is not included.
  • Battery life depends on the update interval: up to 1 year at long intervals, much less at short ones.

FAQ

Can Loko track cattle or sheep?

Only in dry conditions and with a secure mount suited to the animal. Loko is not waterproof and has no ear-tag mount.

Does livestock tracking need cell service?

Cellular tags do. Satellite tags, LoRaWAN gateway tags and Loko do not.

How many animals can one receiver follow?

One Loko Ground follows up to 30 trackers.

Is there a monthly fee?

Not for Loko. You pay once for the hardware.

Loko tracks animals and equipment on your land by radio, with no per-animal fee.
