Tractive Alternative: Which Kind of Tracker Fixes Your Problem?

People look for a Tractive alternative for different reasons. Another cellular brand fixes some of them, but not a coverage problem.

Tractive is a cellular GPS tracker: a built-in SIM sends your dog's position over mobile networks to the app, and you pay a plan for it. That works well where there is signal. If your problem is no signal, the plan, several dogs, charging or size, the right alternative depends on which one it is. This page matches each problem to the kind of tracker that fixes it, and says plainly where Loko fits and where it doesn't.

Updated 24 November 2026. Tractive plans and how Tractive works checked on Tractive's website and help centre on that date; Dogtra price checked on Dogtra's website on that date. Prices change, so check again before you buy.

How Tractive Works, in Its Own Words

Tractive's help centre says all its trackers “contain a built-in, non-exchangeable SIM card”, so you don't buy a SIM. In areas with patchy or no coverage, it says the tracker “will struggle to connect to cell networks and send data back to your app”, and that once it reconnects it sends the positions it recorded while offline to your location history. In short: without signal, you see where your dog was, later.

Tractive's US plans page lists 1-year plans at $108 and $120 a year, 2-year plans at $144 and $168, and a 5-year plan at $300.

When Loko Is the Wrong Choice

  • You want to check on your dog from work or home: that is far beyond radio range. A cellular tracker fits.
  • Your dog swims: Loko is not waterproof; light rain only.
  • You need fast live updates all day: battery life depends on the update interval, up to 12 months at long intervals and much less at short ones.
  • You need alerts that the position is old: the app shows the time of the last position but no warning.

Test Before You Switch

  1. Note where your current tracker loses signal on your usual walks or hunts.
  2. Check how far your dog really ranges from you there.
  3. With Loko, walk that ground with the Ground and check where updates stop, especially behind hills and in trees.
  4. Set the frequency for your region; see the LoRa frequency guide.

FAQ

Does Tractive work without mobile coverage?

Tractive trackers have a built-in SIM and send positions over mobile networks. Tractive says that without coverage the tracker struggles to send data, and once it reconnects it sends the positions it recorded. So you see where the dog was, later, not where it is now.

Is Loko a direct replacement for Tractive?

Not for every job. Loko suits tracking a dog within radio range of a Loko Ground you carry, often where there is no signal, with no plan. Tractive suits seeing your dog from anywhere with mobile coverage, such as from work.

Does Loko have a subscription?

No. The Loko Air sends its position over LoRa radio to the Loko Ground, and the Ground passes it to the Loko app on your phone by Bluetooth. There is no SIM and no plan. A kit is $119 and each extra Air is $59.

How far does Loko reach?

20+ km in open areas with line of sight between the Air and the Ground, and much less among trees, hills and buildings. Beyond that, the app keeps the last position received, with no warning that it is old.

Which costs less for several dogs?

Over 3 years, Tractive's 1-year plan at $108 a year comes to $324 per dog in plan fees, before the tracker itself. Loko is $119 for the first dog and $59 for each extra one, plus an 18650 battery for the Ground, with no plan after that.
