Tractive Alternative Without Subscription

For local tracking where cellular pet trackers and monthly fees are the wrong fit.

Tractive is a well-known pet GPS tracker and can be a good fit for urban dogs with reliable cellular coverage. But it depends on a subscription and mobile networks.

Loko serves a different need: local GPS tracking without SIM cards, GSM, internet, or monthly fees. It is better suited to rural properties, farms, hikes, field training, and off-grid areas.

When Tractive Is the Better Choice

A cellular dog tracker is better if you need location updates when you are far away from the animal and the tracker has reliable mobile coverage.

If your dog is mostly in towns, neighborhoods, and mobile-covered areas, a cellular subscription may be worth paying for.

When Loko Is the Better Choice

Loko is stronger when you are tracking locally in areas with weak or no cellular service. Examples include farms, hunting areas, hiking trails, forests, and camps.

Because there is no cellular plan, Loko also avoids the per-device subscription cost that becomes expensive with multiple dogs or assets.

The Honest Tradeoff

Loko is not a cloud pet tracker. Tractive is not an off-grid LoRa tracker. The right choice depends on whether you need nationwide cloud access or local infrastructure-independent tracking.

FAQ

Is Loko a direct replacement for Tractive?

Not for every use case. Loko is a better alternative when you need local tracking without cellular service or subscriptions.

Does Loko have a monthly fee?

No. Loko does not require a cellular subscription.

Does Tractive work without cell service?

Cellular trackers need mobile coverage to send live location updates.

Which is better for rural dogs?

For local rural tracking without cell coverage, Loko is usually the better fit.
