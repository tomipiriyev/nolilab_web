GPS Tracker With No SIM Card: What “No SIM” Really Means

Some “no SIM” trackers have no SIM at all. Others just hide one inside, along with a plan.

A GPS tracker never needs a SIM to work out where it is: that comes from satellites. A SIM only matters for getting the position to you. So when a listing says “no SIM card needed”, it can mean two very different things: there is no SIM at all, or there is a SIM built in and a plan to pay for.

This page sorts no-SIM trackers into four types, shows what each costs to keep running and where each stops working, and says plainly where Loko fits and where it doesn't.

Updated 17 November 2026. Tractive and Invoxia details checked on their own websites on that date; Garmin prices checked on 13 November 2026. Prices change, so check again before you buy.

Built-in SIM: What the Makers Say

  • Tractive: “All Tractive GPS trackers contain a built-in, non-exchangeable SIM card.” Tracking runs on a Tractive subscription plan.
  • Invoxia GPS Tracker Pro Max: “the SIM is built in”, with 1 year of network subscription included. After that, plans in Europe start at €59.85 a year (€7.95 a month); the tracker costs €179.
  • Garmin inReach (satellite): needs an active plan, from $7.99 a month in the US plus $39.99 activation. The inReach Messenger costs $299.99.

Sources: Tractive Help Center, Invoxia Support, Garmin inReach plans overview.

Read a Listing in Three Questions

  1. How does the position reach my phone? Through nearby phones, a mobile network, your own receiver, or satellites. This tells you which of the four types it is.
  2. Is a plan needed, now or after an included period? “No SIM card needed” and “no subscription” are different promises. Look for what happens after the first year.
  3. Where does it stop working? Every type has a limit: no phones nearby, no mobile coverage, out of radio range, or no view of the sky.

How Loko Works Without a SIM

The 14 g Loko Air gets its position from GPS and sends it over LoRa radio straight to a Loko Ground that you keep with you. The Ground passes it to the Loko app on your phone by Bluetooth. No SIM, no mobile network and no subscription are involved. One Ground can follow up to 30 trackers.

Range is 20+ km in open areas with line of sight, and much less among trees, hills and buildings. You set the radio frequency yourself, between 860 and 926 MHz; see the LoRa frequency guide.

What Loko Shows When the Tracker Is Out of Range

The app keeps the last position it received with the time it arrived, but shows no warning that it is old, so check the time. If the tracker cannot get a satellite fix within 60 seconds, it sends its last known position. Out of range, the Loko Air keeps logging up to 3,100 positions on the device, which you can read later over USB with the configurator. The Ground stores positions while your phone is disconnected, but the app does not pull them in when you reconnect.

When Loko Is the Wrong Choice

  • You need to see the tracker from anywhere in the country, far from your receiver: pick a cellular tracker.
  • You need SOS or messaging: Loko has neither, and no relay or mesh. Pick a satellite communicator or a beacon.
  • The tracker will get wet: Loko is not waterproof, light rain only.
  • You need certified equipment: Loko holds no certifications, so check your local radio rules for the frequency you set.

Battery life depends on the update interval: up to 1 year at long intervals, much less at short ones. The Ground runs on an 18650 battery, which is not included.

FAQ

Can a GPS tracker work without a SIM card?

Yes. Working out a position from GPS satellites needs no SIM. A SIM, built in or not, is only needed when the tracker sends that position over a mobile network.

Does “no SIM card needed” mean no subscription?

Not always. Trackers such as Tractive and Invoxia have a SIM built in, so you don't insert one, but they still need a paid plan once any included period ends.

Does Loko use a SIM or mobile network?

No. The Loko Air sends its position over LoRa radio to the Loko Ground, and the Ground passes it to the app on your phone by Bluetooth. There is no SIM and no subscription.

How far does a no-SIM LoRa tracker reach?

Loko works within radio range of your Loko Ground: 20+ km in open areas with line of sight, and much less among trees, hills and buildings. Beyond that range you see the last position received.

Is Loko an emergency device?

No. Loko has no SOS and no messaging. If you need to call for help far from cell service, use a satellite communicator or a personal locator beacon.
