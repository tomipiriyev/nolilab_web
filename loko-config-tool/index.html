Firmware Update latest bundled

Flash the Loko Ground (ESP32) firmware over USB. The image is written entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Saved settings (ID2, frequency, P2P key) are kept in NVS across updates.

How to enter flash mode
  1. Press and hold the Power button.
  2. Press and hold the IO button.
  3. While holding both IO and Power, press the EN (RESET) button once.
  4. Release the IO button (keep holding Power).
  5. Press Flash Firmware below and select the serial port.
  6. When the progress bar reaches 100%, release the Power button.
  7. Press the EN (RESET) button once. Done.
