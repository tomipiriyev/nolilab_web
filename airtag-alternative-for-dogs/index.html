AirTag Alternative for Dogs

AirTag can help in cities. Loko is built for real GPS tracking away from crowds.

Apple AirTag is small, affordable, and useful for keys and bags. But it is not a GPS tracker. It reports location through nearby Apple devices in the Find My network.

That difference matters for dogs. If your dog runs into a forest, field, farm, or mountain area with no nearby iPhones, an AirTag may not update. Loko uses real GPS on the tracker and LoRa radio back to your receiver.

Why AirTag Is Not a GPS Dog Tracker

AirTag uses Bluetooth and ultra-wideband for nearby finding. For distant location, it depends on other people's Apple devices detecting the tag and reporting that location anonymously.

This is excellent for luggage in airports and keys in a city. It is much less reliable for dogs that may leave the range of people and phones.

Why Real GPS Matters for Dogs

A real GPS tracker receives satellite coordinates on the tracked object itself. It does not need another phone nearby to know where it is.

Loko then sends those GPS coordinates over LoRa radio. This makes it useful for rural dog tracking, farm dogs, hiking, and field training where crowd networks are sparse.

AirTag and Loko Can Serve Different Jobs

AirTag is a good low-cost backup for urban finding. Loko is a stronger fit when you need local active tracking outside cellular and crowd-network coverage.

For the best outcome, match the tracker to the environment instead of only comparing purchase price.

FAQ

Is AirTag safe for dogs?

AirTag can be attached with suitable accessories, but it was not designed as a pet GPS tracker.

Does AirTag have GPS?

No. AirTag does not contain a GPS receiver.

What is the best AirTag alternative for rural dogs?

A real GPS tracker that does not depend on nearby phones is a better fit for rural tracking.

Does Loko need nearby iPhones?

No. Loko uses GPS and LoRa P2P radio, not the Find My network.

Loko GPS Tracker uses GPS plus LoRa radio for local tracking without SIM cards, internet, or monthly subscriptions.
