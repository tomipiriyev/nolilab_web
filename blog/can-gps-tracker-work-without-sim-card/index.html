Can a GPS Tracker Work Without a SIM Card?

Yes. GPS and cellular are different systems, and Loko uses GPS plus LoRa instead of SIM-based data.

A GPS tracker can know its location without a SIM card. GPS satellites provide positioning signals directly to the receiver inside the tracker.

The SIM card is only needed when the tracker uses a cellular network to send that location somewhere else. Loko replaces that cellular step with LoRa radio.

The Common Misunderstanding

People often say GPS when they mean the whole tracking system. In reality, GPS only solves the location part.

A tracker also needs a communication method. Most brands choose cellular. Loko chooses LoRa P2P radio for local offline tracking.

How Loko Sends Location Without a SIM

Loko Air receives its coordinates from GPS, GLONASS, and Galileo satellites. It then transmits those coordinates over LoRa to Loko Ground.

The phone connects to Loko Ground over Bluetooth, so the local tracking workflow does not require internet or a cellular carrier.

When No-SIM Tracking Makes Sense

No-SIM tracking is most useful when you are tracking locally in places with poor cell service or when you want to avoid recurring subscription costs.

FAQ

Does GPS require a SIM card?

No. GPS reception does not require a SIM card.

Why do most GPS trackers have SIM cards?

They use cellular networks to transmit location to an app or cloud server.

How does Loko avoid SIM cards?

Loko uses LoRa P2P radio instead of cellular data.

Does a no-SIM tracker always mean no subscription?

Not always. Some non-SIM network trackers still have service fees. Loko P2P tracking has no cellular subscription.

Loko GPS Tracker uses GPS plus LoRa radio for local tracking without SIM cards, internet, or monthly subscriptions.
