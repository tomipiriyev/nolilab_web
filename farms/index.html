Farm GPS Tracker Without Cell Service

Short answer: Loko shows where your machinery, tools and dogs are within radio range of a Loko Ground you keep on the farm, with no SIM, no plan and no mobile network. It is not an anti-theft system and not built for whole herds.

Range is 20+ km in open areas with line of sight, and much less among trees, hills, sheds and buildings. This page sets out the ways a farm tracker can get a position to you without cell service, what each one misses, and plainly where Loko fits and where it doesn't.

Fit

What Loko Suits on a Farm

  • Machinery and vehicles on your land: tractors, ATVs, trailers and implements, to see where they were left.
  • Tools and movable gear: generators, pumps and containers that move around the property.
  • Working dogs: the 14 g Loko Air is light enough for a dog collar.
  • Several things on one screen: one Ground follows up to 30 trackers.

Where It Is Limited

  • Livestock: Loko is not waterproof (light rain only), has no ear-tag mount, and 30 trackers per Ground is not a whole herd.
  • Safety: Loko has no SOS and no messaging. It is not a safety device for workers.
  • Beyond range: past the Ground's radio range you see only the last position received.

Theft

Loko Is Not an Anti-Theft System

Don't buy Loko to stop theft. It doesn't call or message you, and it only reaches you within radio range of your Ground. A vehicle driven off the farm soon drops out of range, and thieves can find and remove trackers.

What Loko can do is show where your gear was last seen on your own land, with the time it arrived.

How It Works

How Loko Works on a Farm

  1. The Loko Air gets its position from GPS.
  2. It sends the position over LoRa radio to the Loko Ground. You set the radio frequency for your region; see the LoRa frequency guide.
  3. The Ground passes positions to the Loko app on your phone by Bluetooth. No SIM, no mobile network and no plan.

If the Air can't get a fix within 60 seconds, it sends its last known position. When a tracker is out of range, the app keeps the last position with the time it arrived, but shows no warning that it is old, so check the time. The Air keeps logging up to 3,100 positions, which you can read later over USB.

Battery life depends on the update interval: up to 12 months at long intervals, much less at short ones. For gear that sits still for long periods, a long interval makes sense. The Ground runs on an 18650 battery, which is not included.

Range

Test the Range on Your Own Land

  1. Put an Air where it will really live: on the machine, in the shed, on the dog.
  2. Walk or drive the farm with the Ground and note where updates stop, especially behind hills, in trees and inside buildings.
  3. Find a high spot near the house or yard where the Ground sees more of the land.
  4. Repeat with the update interval you plan to use and note the battery use.

FAQ

Questions Farmers Ask

Can you track farm equipment without cell service?

Yes, within radio range of a receiver you keep on the farm. Loko sends each tracker's position over LoRa radio to a Loko Ground, which passes it to the Loko app on your phone by Bluetooth, with no SIM, no mobile network and no plan.

How much of a farm does Loko cover?

Range is 20+ km in open areas with line of sight between the tracker and the Ground, and much less among trees, hills, sheds and buildings. Test it on your own land before you rely on it.

Is Loko good for livestock?

Only in limited cases. Loko is not waterproof (light rain only), has no ear-tag mount, and one Ground follows up to 30 trackers. For a whole herd outdoors in all weather, look at trackers built for livestock.

Will Loko stop theft?

No. Loko is not an anti-theft system. It doesn't call or message you, and it only reaches you within radio range of your Ground, so a vehicle driven away soon drops out of range, and a thief can remove the tracker.

What does Loko cost for a farm?

A kit with one Air and one Ground is $119, and each extra Air is $59, with no plan after that. Ten trackers on one Ground come to $650, plus an 18650 battery for the Ground, which is not included.
