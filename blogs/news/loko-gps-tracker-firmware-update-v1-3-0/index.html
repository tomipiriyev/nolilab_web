Loko GPS Tracker Firmware Update v1.3.0

We are excited to announce firmware v1.3.0 for the Loko Air GPS Tracker. This release focuses on three core improvements that make your tracking experience more reliable, longer-lasting, and more accurate — whether you are monitoring equipment on a large farm, tracking gear in the backcountry, or managing assets across a wide area.

Battery Optimization

Firmware v1.3.0 introduces an improved power-management algorithm that reduces energy consumption during idle and low-activity periods. The new deep-sleep scheduling cuts baseline current draw, delivering up to 30% longer battery life on a single charge. Real-world field tests show typical tracking intervals extending from 21 days to over 28 days with default settings.

  • Optimized deep-sleep duty cycle for lower standby current
  • Smarter GPS fix scheduling that adapts to movement patterns
  • Reduced radio wake time during LoRa transmissions

Improved GPS Accuracy

The updated firmware refines the GPS baseband processing pipeline, resulting in faster satellite acquisition and tighter position fixes. Average accuracy improves from ±2.5 m to ±1.5 m in open-sky conditions. Multi-constellation support (GPS + GLONASS + Galileo) is now enabled by default, giving you more satellites to work with in partially obstructed environments.

  • Faster cold-start satellite acquisition (reduced TTFF)
  • Multi-constellation tracking enabled out of the box
  • Improved filtering for smoother position tracks

Extended LoRa Range

Transmit power calibration and a new adaptive data-rate algorithm push the practical LoRa range further. In line-of-sight testing, v1.3.0 achieves reliable communication at distances up to 5 km — a meaningful improvement for users operating across large properties or in mountainous terrain where every metre of range counts.

  • Recalibrated PA (power amplifier) output for cleaner signal
  • Adaptive spreading-factor selection based on link quality
  • Better performance in high-interference RF environments

How to Update

To upgrade your Loko Air to firmware v1.3.0, follow our step-by-step firmware upgrade guide. Make sure your device is fully charged before starting, and do not disconnect during the update process. The firmware binary is available from the Loko Config Tool page.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed an issue where the device could miss scheduled GPS fixes after a LoRa timeout
  • Resolved a rare crash when switching between P2P and LoRaWAN modes
  • Corrected battery percentage reporting for more accurate level display

Conclusion

Firmware v1.3.0 is a recommended update for all Loko Air users. The combination of longer battery life, better GPS precision, and extended LoRa range makes the tracker more capable than ever. As always, if you encounter any issues, reach out to nolilab support.
